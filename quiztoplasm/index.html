      About The Science Realms

      Quiztoplasm is part of The Science Realms, an interactive revision platform I created and developed during my 2026–2027 gap year. It covers CCEA AS Biology, CCEA AS Chemistry and WJEC AS Psychology. This site is the CCEA AS Biology realm.

      Read about the project and visit all three realms ↗

      How the games help

      • Slide Run gives fast, timed multiple-choice practice for every topic, with an explanation after each answer.
      • Slide Lab uses exam-style and colour diagrams for labelling, sequencing, graph reading and magnification.
      • Molecule Match-Up, DNA Detective, Osmosis Challenge and Cell Surgery make key ideas hands-on: building glucose, pairing bases, calculating water potential and finding faults in cells.
      • Slide Notes give concise key points, exam tips and common mistakes for every topic.

      The Science Realms is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CCEA or WJEC.
