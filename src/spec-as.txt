3.1 Unit AS 1: Molecules and Cells
This unit deals with molecules, enzymes, viruses, cells, cell physiology, continuity of
cells, and tissues and organs. These are core topics in GCE Biology and underpin
many of the other topics involved.
Assessment for this unit consists of a written examination that includes both short
and extended questions. For more details, see Section 6.
