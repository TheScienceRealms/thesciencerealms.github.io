      About The Science Realms

      ChemQuest is part of The Science Realms, an interactive revision platform I created and developed during my 2026–2027 gap year. It covers CCEA AS Biology, CCEA AS Chemistry and WJEC AS Psychology. This site is the CCEA AS Chemistry realm.

      Read about the project and visit all three realms ↗

      How the games help

      • Definition Dash and Definition Builder drill CCEA’s exact definitions, from recognising them at speed to filling the key words.
      • Colour Lab, Flame Test Lab and the Ionic Equation Builder teach precise observations and the equations behind them.
      • Practical Builder and Mark Scheme Mechanic build 6-mark answers step by step and mark them against indicative content and CCEA bands.
      • Shape Shifter, Electron Pair Escape, Bond Angle Blitz and Dipole Detective use rotatable 3D molecules to teach shapes and polarity.
      • Topic Quizzes and the Section A Bank give CCEA-style multiple-choice practice, and the Lab Book pages give concise notes and exam tips for every topic.

      The Science Realms is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CCEA or WJEC.
