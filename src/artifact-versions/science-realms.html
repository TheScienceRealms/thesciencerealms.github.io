About The Science Realms

The Science Realms is an interactive revision platform for CCEA AS Biology, CCEA AS Chemistry and WJEC AS Psychology. I created and developed it as an independent project during my 2026–2027 gap year.

Why I made it

As a student who studied A levels in Northern Ireland, I know how hard it is to find engaging, accessible revision resources made for our own exam board, CCEA. Students taking the English boards, such as AQA, OCR and Edexcel, have a huge range of websites, videos and games to choose from. For CCEA there is very little, so we often end up using material that doesn’t match our specifications, terminology or exam style.

I built The Science Realms to help change that: revision that is interactive, enjoyable and effective, with games designed around the actual CCEA specifications.

I studied triple award science at GCSE, then Biology, Chemistry and Psychology at A level, as well as French at AS level. I know how it feels to work hard and still not get the grade you were aiming for. I built The Science Realms to help other students aim higher and reach their full potential, because nothing pays off more than results day.

My AS year showed me how crucial strong foundations are. Getting the core knowledge and exam technique secure at AS is what allowed me to achieve what I wanted at A level. In the sciences, that meant 91% in AS Chemistry and 84% in AS Biology, alongside 95% in AS Psychology. This platform is built to help other students lay those same foundations.

What it covers

CCEA GCE AS Biology (Units AS 1 and AS 2), CCEA GCE AS Chemistry (Units AS 1 and AS 2) and WJEC GCE AS Psychology (Units 1 and 2). Content is written to match each specification and the style of recent papers and mark schemes. All questions are original.

The aim

To make challenging concepts more accessible through interactive learning: practising with the exact exam-board terminology, exam technique and mark-scheme thinking, rather than only reading notes.

The Science Realms is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CCEA or WJEC. Scores are saved in each student’s own browser only.
