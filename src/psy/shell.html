      About The Science Realms

      The Psycheverse is part of The Science Realms, an interactive revision platform I created and developed during my 2026–2027 gap year. It covers CCEA AS Biology, CCEA AS Chemistry and WJEC AS Psychology. This site is the WJEC AS Psychology realm.

      Read about the project and visit all three realms ↗

      How the games help

      • Approach Arena, Therapy Lab and Relationship Builder sort the five approaches, their therapies and their explanations of relationship formation.
      • Case Files and Fact Checks drill the classic research, from methodology to ethics.
      • Answer Builder plans, writes and self-marks 2 to 10-mark answers against indicative content and WJEC-style levels, and Describe vs Evaluate trains the command words.
      • Debate Arena, model essays and 20/20 plans prepare the five contemporary debates.
      • Method Match, Sampling Sort, Scenario Lab, the Ethics Committee and the Standard Deviation Lab build Section B research methods skills, and Mind Notes give concise notes and exam tips for every topic.

      The Science Realms is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CCEA or WJEC.
